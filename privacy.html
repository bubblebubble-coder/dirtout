Privacy Policy – DiRT OUT Bot

Last updated: 3 November 2026

DiRT OUT Bot is an internal tool used only by DiRT OUT (JB Shoes Cleaning Shop) to publish its own marketing posts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Google Photos (Picker API): only the photos the owner explicitly selects each week, used to create one before/after image.
  • Google Business Profile: used only to publish updates to DiRT OUT's own business locations.

How data is used and stored

Selected photos are edited (cropped, logo added) and published to DiRT OUT's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and Google Business Profile. Files and access tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI model training.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local copies are deleted on request.

Google API data use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
